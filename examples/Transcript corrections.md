# Transcript corrections

Words Whisper keeps getting wrong. The transcriber fixes them in every new transcript.

- One per line, as `- wrong -> right`.
- Matching ignores case and only matches whole words.
- `## All` applies to every course. A `## CODE` section applies to that course only.
- A line starting with `%%` is ignored.
